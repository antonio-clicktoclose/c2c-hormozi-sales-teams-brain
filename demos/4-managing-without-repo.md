## What's broken

**1. Show rate (50%) is the biggest leak. Fix this first.**

Half of your booked meetings disappear. For cold outbound, a decent show rate is 65% to 75%. Three things are almost certainly causing it:

- **Booking 5 to 8 days out is too far.** A cold prospect who said yes on Tuesday has forgotten you by next Wednesday. Interest fades fast with outbound because they didn't come looking for you.
- **Setters are paid for time, not results.** At $18/hour flat, nothing rewards a setter for booking someone who actually shows up. You get a lot of soft "sure, send me an invite" bookings.
- **Weak confirmation process (probably).** If the only reminder is an automated calendar invite, a lot of people will ghost.

**2. Connect-to-conversation (21%) is the second weakest point.**

About 4 out of 5 people who pick up hang up within 3 minutes. That points to the opener and the list. Either the first 15 seconds don't give a reason to keep listening, or you're calling people who don't fit (wrong role, wrong size, no budget for $3,500/month).

**3. Close rate (21% of held calls) is okay, not great.**

For a $3,500/month retainer, 20% to 30% of held calls is normal. Fix the show rate before you blame the closer. Weak bookings that do show up also drag down close rate.

## What to fix first: the show rate

Here's why it comes first. If you move show rate from 50% to 70% with the same number of bookings, you go from 19 held calls to about 27. At your current close rate, that's about 5 to 6 deals instead of 4. That's an extra $5,000 to $7,000 a month in new revenue with zero extra dials.

**Do these this week:**

- **Book within 48 hours.** Offer "today or tomorrow" times first. Open more closer slots if needed. You can take some of those calls yourself. If a prospect can't do it within 3 days, treat it as a weaker lead.
- **Have the setter confirm on the call.** Before hanging up, the setter should say the time back to them, ask them to accept the invite while they're still on the phone, and get a mobile number for a text.
- **Add a human confirmation sequence.** Send a personal text from the setter right after booking. Then send a reminder the day before and one about 1 to 2 hours before. A short video or a one-line "here's what we'll cover" message also helps.
- **Change setter pay.** Keep a lower base, around $14 to $15/hour, and add a bonus per *held* meeting ($30 to $50), plus a bonus per close. Pay on shows, not on bookings. That one change tells them exactly what you want.
- **Track show rate by setter.** If one setter is at 65% and the other is at 35%, you have a people problem, not a process problem.

## Fix second: the first 30 seconds of the call

- Listen to 20 recorded calls that ended under 3 minutes. Find where people drop off.
- Tighten the opener so it gives a specific reason to talk, tied to a problem they have.
- Check the list. Make sure you're calling owners or decision makers at businesses that can afford $42K a year.

## Then: the close

Once more qualified people are showing up, review the closer's recorded calls. Look at how they handle price and "let me think about it." Also check whether you close at a different rate than your closer. If you do, find out what you do differently and write it down.

## One more thing to track

Start measuring **how many days out each meeting was booked** next to whether it showed. I'd expect meetings booked within 2 days to show far more often than ones booked a week out. That data will tell you how hard to push on fast booking.
