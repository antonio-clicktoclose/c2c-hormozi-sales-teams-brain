Start by writing down how you sell. Don't start with the job post. In the course, the step before any hire is a **frame** and a **script**. The frame is what the buyer sees before the call. The script is what gets said on the call. Without them, a new rep has no standard to hit.

## What I'm assuming

You didn't share your numbers, so I've assumed four things:

- You close most or all sales yourself today.
- Your sales happen on calls (phone or video).
- You get a steady flow of leads.
- You have no written script yet.

If any of these is wrong, tell me. Also send me what you sell, the price, your leads per month, and your close rate. Then I can make each step fit your business.

## Step 1: Prove the process, then add the person

- **Never two news.** Don't put a new person on a new process. If sales drop, you won't know which one failed. Your own calls are the proof that the process works. [M2 0:25](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=4533)
- **Founder sales work because the founder does all the basics.** The job is to get a rep to do the same basics. [M1 9:55](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=597)

## Step 2: Write the script (this is your first fix)

- **No script, no sales team.** Hormozi tried "just do what I do" in his gyms. It failed until a partner wrote the script down and taught it. [M1 10:47](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=651)
- **"No one can sell like me."** His answer: a rep can't sell *like* you, but they can sell *as well as* you. [M1 12:58](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=780)
- **The script defines good.** Without it, reps aim at a target they can't see. [M1 16:01](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=963)
- **Use four parts: intro, discovery, pitch, close.** [M1 28:51](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=1735)
  - **Intro:** your name, the company, why you're calling, the agenda, and a small yes ("Sound good?").
  - **Discovery:** where the buyer is now versus where they want to be. Dig into the pain. Then recap and confirm: "So the real issue is X. Does that sound right?" This is the most important part. [M1 32:00](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=1922)
  - **Pitch:** two minutes at most. Use problem, solution, benefit, three times, and confirm each one. [M1 35:48](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=2150)
  - **Close:** state the price, ask, then stop talking. Hormozi says his reps who wait at least eight seconds close 30% more. That's his team's number, not a promise. [M1 37:50](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=2275)
- **Keep it short.** In most scripts his team reviews, they cut 50 to 75%, and sales go up. [M1 30:00](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=1801)

**Why this comes first:** each later step depends on it. You interview against the script. You train with it. You judge a rep by how closely they follow it.

## Step 3: Build a simple frame

- **The frame is what buyers see before the call.** Today the most scalable version is a VSL (video sales letter). A buyer who already knows more needs fewer and shorter calls. [M1 17:29](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=1050)
- **The VSL outline:** promise, pain, proof, plan, picture, old way versus new way, FAQ, call to action, then a run of customer results. [M1 19:59](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=1202)
- Start with one VSL. A simple version can be 10 minutes of before-and-after examples. [M1 24:54](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=1495)

## Step 4: Decide who to hire first

- **Few leads, so you must create demand:** hire a setter first. A setter books the calls. **Many leads, and you close them:** hire a closer first. [M2 6:55](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=4921) If you want to hand off the sales calls themselves, that seat is usually a closer.
- **Look for the three Ps plus one.** Has the person sold to this type of buyer, in this type of process, for this type of product? You want two of the three. If you can only weigh one, pick the buyer. Also look for at least one year of experience. [M2 1:23](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=4591)
- **Three traits:** they learn fast from feedback, they work hard, and they're humble. A rep who wants to change your script before they sell is a bad sign. [M2 3:20](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=4707)
- **Hire more than one if your cash flow allows.** Training five takes as long as training one. If all five fail, you know the process is the problem. [M2 0:46](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=4550)
- **Be careful about hiring a sales leader first.** It's the fastest path, but it's easy to be fooled by a good salesperson. [M2 8:53](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=5041)

## Step 5: Set the pay from the math

- **Pay on closes,** not on booked or attended calls. [M2 14:48](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=5395)
- **Split on-target earnings 50/50.** On-target earnings (OTE) is what a rep earns at target. Half is base pay and half is variable pay. [M2 15:56](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=5461)
- **Work out a flat rate per sale.** In his example, a $100,000 OTE gives $50,000 of variable pay. That's about $1,000 a week over 50 weeks. At 12 closes a week, that's about $83 per sale. These are his numbers. Run the same math with your OTE and your expected closes. [M2 17:21](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=5550)

Pay levels and employment rules depend on where you operate. The course gives no pay level for a given industry.

## Step 6: Train before you hand over leads

- **Bootcamp first.** New reps spend 14 days on script memorization, role play, recorded calls and buyer training before they work the full lead flow. [M3 0:00](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=6938)
- **Read the script out loud 25 times a day.** [M3 4:23](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7203)
- **Ramp gates.** Cap the volume a new rep gets, so they don't burn your leads while they learn. [M3 10:33](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7571)
- **Say on day one that this seat must perform within 60 days.** [M3 14:50](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7830)

For the full onboarding plan, ask me about training. The `sales-team-3-training` skill covers it.

## What to do this week, in order

1. **Record your next sales calls.** Pick the 3 to 5 calls that closed. (Not from the training: this is my suggestion for how to get raw material for the script.)
2. **Write the script from those calls** in four parts: intro, discovery, pitch, close. Keep the pitch under two minutes.
3. **Cut the script.** Remove anything that doesn't move the buyer to the next part.
4. **Run your own next calls from the written script.** This proves it works when you follow it word for word.
5. **List the top five objections you hear,** with your answer to each one. These go into the script and the VSL FAQ.
6. **Send me your offer, price, leads per month and close rate.** Then I can tell you whether to hire a setter or a closer first, and work out the pay per sale with your numbers.
