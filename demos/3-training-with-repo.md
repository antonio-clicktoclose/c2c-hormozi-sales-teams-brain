# Your new setter's first 14 days

Run the first two weeks as a closed bootcamp. Keep your new setter away from the rest of the team and away from your full lead list. Week 1 is about learning the script and your buyer. Week 2 moves them onto the phones, with a cap on how many leads they can touch. This is the 14-day bootcamp from the training. [M3 0:25](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=6962)

## My assumptions

You didn't give me your numbers, so this plan assumes the following. Tell me if any of them are wrong and I'll adjust the plan.

- You hire one phone setter who books calls for a closer (you or someone on your team).
- You already have a written setter script. **If you don't, write it before Monday.** The whole bootcamp is built on memorizing a script. Use the `sales-team-1-strategy` skill for that.
- You (or your sales manager) can give this person about three short role-play sessions every day for two weeks.
- Leads come in by phone, text and email. If you sell in person, the same plan works: "dials" become leads worked, and "live listens" become ride-alongs.

To make the plan sharper, send me: what you sell and the price, leads per month, inbound or outbound, and your current show rate and close rate.

## Why a bootcamp and not "sit next to Bob"

Hormozi's team used to drop new reps into the team. They got random tips from everyone and felt overwhelmed. Now they keep new reps apart for 14 days of full-day training. [M3 0:00](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=6938)

Each day mixes five things: game tape (recorded calls), script memorization, role play, the handbook and prospect training. [M3 1:17](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7017)

Spend more time on your buyer than on your product. Teach their pains and the words they use. The pitch itself is only about two minutes of the call. [M3 1:21](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7021)

## Daily habits for all 14 days (from the course)

- **Read the script out loud 25 times a day.** That's about two hours. Hormozi says boredom means you're doing it right. One of his setters did 50 reads a day and learned the script faster than anyone. [M3 4:23](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7203)
- **Three role plays a day with the manager.** [M3 2:21](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7080)
- **Blackout method:** each time they read a printed copy of the script, black out one more word. [M3 6:43](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7342)

## Week 1: learn the script (days 0 to 7)

The day topics come from Hormozi's plan. [M3 2:40](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7097) [M3 2:54](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7110) [M3 3:24](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7142) The "owner" and "pass check" columns are my proposal for your business.

| Day | Focus (course) | Owner | Pass check (my proposal) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (this weekend) | The team reaches out. One-on-one call. Homework: read the script and your buyer notes. | You | They arrive Monday having read the script at least once. |
| 1 (Mon) | Sell them the process: your values, career path, ramp and gates. Tell them it's a 60-day performance seat. | You | They can repeat back the gates and the 60-day rule. |
| 2 (Tue) | Intro and the first friction points on the call. | Manager | Opening delivered word for word in role play. |
| 3 (Wed) | Discovery. This is the heaviest day. "The pain is the pitch." | Manager | They find the buyer's main pain in 3 role plays in a row. |
| 4 (Thu) | Bridge, pitch and booking. Book the next meeting from inside the call. | Manager | They book the call in role play without skipping a step. |
| 5 (Fri) | Full script under pressure. | Manager | Full script start to finish, no notes. |
| 6 (Sat or next working day) | First real dials. | Setter, manager listens | Calls made. Manager gives one piece of feedback per call. |
| 7 | **Script graduation: pass or fail.** | You and the manager | Passes the rubric below. |

How to run each role play: frame it (what, how, why), the manager models it, the setter copies it, they practice until it's right, then recap. "There's only two ways to train, modeling and role play." [M3 5:22](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7260)

In week 1, the manager plays an easy prospect with scripted answers until the whole script is automatic. After that, run speed rounds. [M3 6:11](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7310)

### Day 7 graduation rubric

The four checks are from the course. [M3 2:54](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7110) The scoring is my proposal.

| Check | Pass |
|---|---|
| Script adherence | Every section in order, close to word for word |
| Tonality | Questions sound like questions. Pace matches the prospect. |
| Recaps | Repeats the prospect's pain back in the prospect's own words |
| Top five objections | Handles each of your top five objections from memory |

Score each check 1 to 5. Pass = 4 or more on every check (my proposal). If they fail, retest on day 9. If they fail again, use the cut rule below.

## Week 2: move to the phones (days 8 to 14)

From the course: more time on the phones, shadowing live calls and reviewing game tape. [M3 9:23](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7502)

- **Day 8 survey.** Send a private message: what should we start, stop and continue? Hormozi fixes a pattern in the playbook. He handles one rep's issue one-on-one. [M3 8:13](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7431) With one new setter you don't have a pattern yet. Save the answers and compare them with your next hire (my adaptation).
- **Days 8 to 14 daily split (my proposal):** morning script reads and one role play, then dials under Gate 1, a midday role play on that morning's worst call, then afternoon dials and one game tape review.
- **Day 14 check (my proposal):** they hit the Gate 1 cap each day, and you have heard at least five of their real calls.

## Ramp gates: do not give them all your leads

Hormozi had a new rep burn through leads at 50 connects a day. No one showed up to the calls. The gates protect your leads. [M3 9:54](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7529)

These are his team's numbers, not a target for your market. [M3 10:33](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7571)

- **Gate 1:** cap at 30 connects a day. On his team that gives about 10 real conversations (3 minutes or more) and 2 to 3 booked calls. Hold this for about a week, or roughly 10 booked calls.
- **Gate 2:** cap at 3 booked calls a day until the show rate is above 60%. Aim for 70 to 75%. [M3 10:58](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7595)
- **Fully ramped (his targets):** 12 or more booked calls a week, 70% or higher show rate, within two months. Celebrate in public each time they clear a gate. [M3 11:21](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7620)

So in your first two weeks, expect your setter to be on Gate 1 only. If your lead flow is small, 30 connects a day may be more than you have. Then the cap is simply "your leads, worked well."

**Dial cadence.** His team cut from 14 to 7 dials per lead because the last seven brought in only about 10% of sales. Their pattern: 3 double dials on day 1, 2 on day 2, 1 on day 3, 1 on day 4, plus text and email. An email reply triggers a call at once. [M3 11:50](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7648) Check your own texting and calling rules where you operate before you copy this.

## Tell them the keep-or-cut rule on day 1

- Say it on day 1: this is a performance seat with 60 days to prove it. [M3 14:50](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7830)
- One week behind during ramp = a two-week coaching plan with clear targets. Bad culture fit, or they can't take feedback = cut now. [M3 14:14](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7794)
- Give feedback on behavior you can observe ("you talk too fast and your voice doesn't rise on questions"), not on labels ("you're not confident"). [M3 14:24](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7801)
- The best early red flag: on day 1, they try to improve your script. [M3 15:22](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=7861)

Day 60 rule (my wording of the course rule): if they're not at or near the fully ramped targets for your business by day 60, they leave. Extend only for a rare rep who is very coachable and works hard.

## Show them the path up (on day 1)

Promotion to closer is far past the first two weeks. But you sell it on day 1. A setter becomes eligible when they hold all KPI minimums for a full quarter, with 100% admin compliance and no repeat process mistakes. Eligible is not promoted: they must be "undeniable." [M3 19:52](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=8130) For pay bumps at each step, use the `sales-team-2-recruiting` skill.

## What to do this week, in order

1. **Check your script.** If there's no written setter script, write it before Monday.
2. **Block your calendar** for three role plays a day for the next two weeks. Name the manager if it isn't you.
3. **Write a one-page buyer sheet:** your top buyer types, their pains, the words they use and your top five objections.
4. **Pull 3 to 5 recorded calls** (your best ones) for game tape.
5. **Write down Gate 1 and the 60-day rule** so you can hand them over on day 1.
6. **This weekend (day 0):** call your setter, send the script and buyer sheet as homework.
