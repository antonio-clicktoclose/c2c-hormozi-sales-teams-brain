# What's broken in your outbound funnel, and the first fix

**Short answer:** your biggest leak is the show rate. Half your meetings don't happen. The most likely cause is the 5 to 8 day gap between booking and the call. The training says to book within four days at most, and same day or next day is best. Fix that first.

## Your numbers, step by step

I checked that your numbers agree. They do. $1.1M a year is about $91,700 a month, which is about 26 clients at $3,500. Four new clients a month adds $14,000 in monthly retainer.

## Where the deals are lost

**1. Show rate: 19 of 38 meetings did not happen.** This is the step with the clearest rule you are breaking.

- The training lists the usual causes of a low show rate: poor qualification, too much time between booking and the call, a bad time of day, or the prospect did not watch the pre-call video (VSL). Slow speed to lead and a closer who is slow to open the three-way text thread (prospect, setter and closer, opened right after the set) also hurt. [M4 26:57](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=10105) [M4 27:30](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=10140) [M4 27:51](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=10160)
- **Booking rule:** book within four days at most. Same day or next day is best for both show rate and close rate. You book 5 to 8 days out. [M4 28:06](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=10175)
- For an outbound team like yours, "speed" means the time from the set to the closer's first touch. [M4 27:51](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=10160)

The math with your numbers: each show turns into a client about 21% of the time (4 / 19). So each extra show is worth about 0.21 clients, or about $737 a month in retainer. If you got 10 more points of show rate (38 x 10% = 3.8 more shows), that is about 0.8 more clients a month, or about $2,800 more in monthly retainer, with no extra dials. That 10 points is my illustration, not a training target.

**2. Qualification may be loose.** HYPOTHESIS: your setters turn 62% of conversations into meetings, but half of those don't show. That pattern can mean some meetings are set with people who don't meet BANT. Check this on game tape before you act on it. [M4 26:57](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=10105)

His team's BANT:

- **Budget:** enough cash flow to invest. They don't set people who can't invest. [M4 28:13](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=10180)
- **Authority:** can make the decision. If not, get every decision maker on the next call. For an agency deal, ask about a partner or co-owner. [M4 28:32](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=10200)
- **Need:** a strong desired state in their words, plus a real obstacle. [M4 28:38](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=10205)
- **Timing:** they want to solve it now. [M4 28:47](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=10215)

**3. Setter pay is not tied to closes.** Your setters earn $18 an hour flat. Nothing in their pay changes when a meeting shows up or closes. The training says to pay setters and closers on closes, not on sets or shows, so everyone works toward the same goal. [M2 14:48](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=5395) He also splits on-target earnings (OTE) about 50% base and 50% variable. [M2 15:56](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=5461)

Assumption: 40 hours a week, about 173 hours a month. Then each setter costs about $3,100 a month, so about $6,200 for both. That is about $164 per meeting set and about $1,560 in setter pay per new client. Fix this second, not first. A pay change takes longer to plan, and pay rules depend on where you operate.

**4. Close rate: 4 of 19.** The training says a qualified prospect who shows up and doesn't buy is one of three problems: a weak offer, a prospect who isn't ready to buy, or the closer's selling skill (intro, discovery, pitch or close). [M4 45:32](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=11221) [M4 46:02](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=11250) Don't work on this yet. A long booking gap hurts close rate too, so fix the gap first and then see where close rate settles. [M4 28:06](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=10175)

**5. Connects and conversations.** You get 290 connects from 4,100 dials. The training calls a low connect rate usually a system issue: spam flags, recycled numbers, wrong time of day, worked-out lists. The rep issue is skipped dials. [M4 24:23](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=9951) For connect to conversation, it says to game tape the first 30 seconds of 10 to 30 calls. [M4 25:26](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=10015) Without a target or your past months, I can't call these broken. Track them by month.

## The one first fix

**Book every meeting within four days. Make same day or next day the default.**

Why this one first:

- It is the biggest drop in your funnel. You lose half your sets at this step.
- It breaks a clear rule in the training, so it is not a guess.
- It costs nothing and you can start tomorrow.
- It can lift show rate and close rate at the same time.

## What to do this week, in order

1. **Today: change the booking rule.** Setters offer today or tomorrow first. Nothing goes past four days. Open the closer's calendar so the near slots exist. You may need to take more calls yourself this month. [M4 28:06](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=10175)
2. **Today: open a three-way text right after each set.** Prospect, setter and closer in one thread. The closer replies fast. [M4 26:57](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=10105)
3. **Tomorrow: book calls at times the prospect can focus,** not while driving or between client meetings. [M4 27:30](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=10140)
4. **Midweek: game tape 10 set calls,** 5 that showed and 5 that didn't. Check each against BANT. This tests the qualification hypothesis above. [M4 26:57](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=10105)
5. **End of week: track show rate by days-to-meeting** (0 to 1 day, 2 to 4 days, 5+ days). After two to four weeks, you will see if the short gap works for you.
6. **Next: draft a setter pay plan tied to closes,** with a base and a per-close bonus from your own OTE math. Check local pay rules before you change it. [M2 14:48](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=5395) [M2 17:21](https://youtu.be/oVPXgRs0IdI?t=5550)

Hormozi's rules come from his own team's results. Your market wins where they differ. Test each change against your own numbers.
