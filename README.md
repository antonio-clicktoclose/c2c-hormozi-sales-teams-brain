# Hormozi $100M Sales Teams brain for Claude (free)

Alex Hormozi gives away his whole sales team system for free: a 3-hour training on YouTube, and the same training as a 4-video course on acquisition.com. This repo turns it into Claude skills. Ask Claude about your own sales team, and it answers with Hormozi's frameworks, does the math with your numbers, and links every answer to the minute of the training it came from.

Not made by or affiliated with Alex Hormozi or Acquisition.com. It is a summary in our own words. Watch the originals:
- The course: https://www.acquisition.com/salesstart
- The same training as one video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oVPXgRs0IdI

## Use it

**Claude Code (recommended)**
1. Download this repo (green Code button, then Download ZIP) and unzip it, or `git clone` it.
2. Open the folder in Claude Code, at claude.ai/code or with `claude` in a terminal.
3. Ask about your sales team. Claude picks the right skill on its own.

**Claude app (claude.ai or desktop)**
1. Zip one skill folder from `.claude/skills/`, for example `sales-team-builder`.
2. In Claude, open Settings, then Capabilities, then Skills, and upload the zip.

## Try these

- "I close every deal myself and get 120 inbound leads a month. Who do I hire first, and how do I pay them?"
- "My setters booked 38 meetings, 19 showed, we closed 4. What do I fix first?"
- "Review my onboarding plan against Hormozi's bootcamp." (paste your plan)

Give it your numbers: what you sell, the price, leads per month, who sells today, and your show and close rates. More in `prompts/`.
