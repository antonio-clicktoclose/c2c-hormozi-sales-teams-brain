# 06. Managing the team: culture, the manager's day, huddles, one-on-ones

## Culture of feedback and reinforcement

- **Clean the team when needed.** He once cut a gym-launch team from 14 to 5 and sales went up. The A players step up. [M4 0:26 · YT 2:21:55] [M4 0:35 · YT 2:22:03]
- **Culture is the most fragile thing in a sales department.** No single rep is worth it. The hardest cut is the bad person who makes you money. [M4 1:03 · YT 2:22:30]
- **The worst person on the team is your real standard.** If the team is mediocre, look at the leader. [M4 1:32 · YT 2:23:00] [M4 1:37 · YT 2:23:06]
- Tie each behavior to the rep's personal goals, and their goals to the company vision. [M4 2:03 · YT 2:23:30]
- **Closers still do 2 hours of outbound per day,** at the best pickup time, for double commission on those deals. It keeps them sharp and respectful of setters. Falling self-sets are a leading sign that a closer is getting lazy. [M4 2:53 · YT 2:24:23] [M4 3:30 · YT 2:25:01]
- **Written handbook.** Expectations, mindset, stories. Every time a good rep quits, add the reason to the handbook. His is 26,000 words. [M4 4:02 · YT 2:25:30] [M4 5:32 · YT 2:27:02]
- **Leaderboards:** visible, accurate, real time, with real outcomes (bonus, shout-outs, a belt). Start with an automated chat message on every close. [M4 6:09 · YT 2:27:38] [M4 6:33 · YT 2:28:02]
- **Five daily non-negotiables** (both teams): 20 minutes of company content, one role play, one game tape or shadow, a workout, and an outbound block (closers) or 50 pipeline dials (SDRs). Reps post a screenshot in the team thread. [M4 7:02 · YT 2:28:30] [M4 7:30 · YT 2:29:00]
- They cap dialers at two lines because their leads are valuable. Cheap leads can use more lines. [M4 7:30 · YT 2:29:00]

## The manager's day (his sample calendar)

## The daily kickoff (huddle)

- Hold it at the slowest call time of day so you do not waste the best hours. [M4 15:20 · YT 2:36:49]
- **One theme per day, matching the script:** Monday intro, Tuesday discovery, Wednesday pitch, Thursday close, Friday hot topic (the week's sticking point, or popcorn role play), Saturday pipeline review (5 to 10 minutes). He calls this "final form". Sales is a perishable skill. Only skip if a close call runs late. [M4 15:42 · YT 2:37:11] [M4 16:14 · YT 2:37:42] [M4 16:49 · YT 2:38:18] [M4 17:08 · YT 2:38:36]

## Weekly one-on-ones

- His SDR targets: 50 sets a month, 70%+ show rate, 10+ closes a month, about five sets per close. Above 13 deals a month unlocks the top commission tier. [M4 20:14 · YT 2:41:43] [M4 20:34 · YT 2:42:02]
- The rep fills a template at least 30 minutes before. Not preparing is a cultural red flag. The rep brings 1 to 2 calls; the manager also hunts for bad calls. [M4 20:44 · YT 2:42:10] [M4 21:14 · YT 2:42:44]
- **Structure:** personal check-in first; last week's focus and whether the number moved; data versus last week and the team average; one focus for this week (one KPI and the behavior that drives it); written action items with due dates. [M4 21:36 · YT 2:43:05]
- Focus on one thing, not 10, even if 10 things are wrong. [M4 22:24 · YT 2:43:53]
- When a rep has crushed it for three months, switch the one-on-one to long-term goals and give them a growth opportunity. [M4 54:44 · YT 3:16:13]

## The closer's day: hunt mode and kill mode

- **Hunt mode** = off the call (prepare, follow up, set). **Kill mode** = on the call. Define what a closer does every hour, on or off a call. [M4 30:20 · YT 2:51:48] [M4 30:31 · YT 2:52:00]
- **Call notes:** a short note from each call transcript, pasted in the CRM, so the next person knows the full story. Review them the night before and the morning of calls. (Hormozi offers a free Call Notes skill for this.) [M4 31:01 · YT 2:52:29] [M4 32:00 · YT 2:53:28]
- **Work the list in priority order:** (1) inbound sets not yet spoken to: call at once and try to pull the call forward; (2) "book a meeting from a meeting" follow-ups; (3) pipeline: no-shows and stalled deals, newest to oldest, 60 days back. He says there is almost always about a deal a day hiding in the existing list. [M4 32:32 · YT 2:54:01] [M4 33:01 · YT 2:54:29] [M4 33:31 · YT 2:55:00]
- **Pull forward.** Calling a booked lead to do the call now raises show rate, close rate and rep utilization, and removes reminder work. [M4 33:31 · YT 2:55:00] [M4 34:33 · YT 2:56:02]
- **Confirmation sequence** for unconfirmed bookings (12 hours, 24 hours before, then cancel and rebook). A no-show is not a no: "looks like we got our wires crossed", offer two times. [M4 35:38 · YT 2:57:06] [M4 36:40 · YT 2:58:08]
- **Setter to closer handoff:** the setter sends the material; the closer messages within minutes; send a testimonial from someone with the same problem and highlight the relevant part. [M4 37:03 · YT 2:58:30] [M4 37:46 · YT 2:59:16]
- **Value ladder** for ghosted or stalled deals: value message, check-in question, bump, re-offer. [M4 38:36 · YT 3:00:06]
- **Outbound block (2 hours):** first referrals from current and past customers (compliment, intro, give them the words), then unscheduled opt-ins. He says over 30% of sales can come from referrals. [M4 39:39 · YT 3:01:07] [M4 40:33 · YT 3:02:02] [M4 41:31 · YT 3:03:00]
- **End-of-day checklist (about 60 minutes):** CRM outcomes, call notes, submit the worst call for review, inbox zero. [M4 42:35 · YT 3:04:03] [M4 43:32 · YT 3:05:00]
- Closer daily max: 20 scheduled calls, which usually means 12 to 15 live calls. [M4 44:45 · YT 3:06:14]

## Monthly full-team meeting (30 minutes)

- Agenda, team-to-team kudos, last month's wins, top three kudos, belt ceremony for number one, improvements for next month, what is coming next in the company. [M4 55:21 · YT 3:16:49] [M4 55:32 · YT 3:17:02]
- Promotions and the belt are the two biggest reinforcing events. Say exactly which behaviors earned them. [M4 55:50 · YT 3:17:18] [M4 56:05 · YT 3:17:34]
- Set career milestones on total lifetime volume (100, 300, 500 deals), not time served. [M4 59:22 · YT 3:20:51]
