# 07. Diagnose the numbers: root cause and fix

Source: the manager "cheat sheet" for SDRs [M4 23:32 · YT 2:45:00] to [M4 29:32 · YT 2:51:00] and the closer fixes [M4 45:17 · YT 3:06:44] to [M4 53:32 · YT 3:15:00]. Use this file when the owner gives a weak metric. About one minute here (2:47:30 to 2:48:30) has no YouTube captions. Those rows also cite the same passage in the $100M Sales Teams course, module 4 (M4), at acquisition.com. The training gives a target only for some steps (show rate, sets, closes). For connect rate, conversation rate and close rate it gives causes and fixes, not benchmarks. Do not invent one; compare the owner to their own past months instead.

## Setter (SDR) funnel

| Metric is low | Likely root cause | His fix | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dials made | Not enough dial time: poor scheduling, slow dialing | Review activity, set clear dial blocks, coach time management. No "victory laps" after a set. | [M4 23:43 · YT 2:45:12] [M4 24:11 · YT 2:45:38] |
| Connect rate | Usually a system issue: spam flags, recycled numbers, slow speed to lead, wrong time of day, over-worked lists. Rep issue: skipped dials | 1) find the best time of day, 2) dial sprints at that time, 3) check for skipped dials, 4) check the dialer and the lists | [M4 24:23 · YT 2:45:51] [M4 24:33 · YT 2:46:03] [M4 25:02 · YT 2:46:30] |
| Connect to conversation (3+ minutes) | Weak top of call: flat tone, no clear ask, early objections not handled | Game tape the first 30 seconds of 10 to 30 calls, role play the ask and early objections | [M4 25:26 · YT 2:46:55] [M4 25:34 · YT 2:47:03] |
| Conversation to meeting set | A discovery issue: no strong desired state, or no clear obstacle, so no urgency. The rep never gets the prospect to say clearly where they want to be | Game tape to find the moment the desired state was missed, and role play drawing it out. If there is a desired state but no obstacle, game tape where the rep stops digging, and role play asking what is in the way until a real obstacle comes up | [M4 26:06 · YT 2:47:34, no YouTube captions] |
| Show rate | Poor qualification, too much time between booking and the call, a bad time of day, or the VSL was not watched. Also slow speed to lead, or the closer is slow to open the three-way message (a text thread with the prospect, the setter and the closer, opened right after the set, so the prospect sees the team is on it) | Check BANT on the call; book same day or next day and make that the default; book a time they can focus, not while driving; faster speed to lead; closer joins the thread fast. For outbound teams, read speed as the time from the set to the closer's first touch | [M4 26:57 · YT 2:48:25, no YouTube captions] [M4 27:14 · YT 2:48:42] [M4 27:30 · YT 2:49:00] [M4 27:51 · YT 2:49:20] |

- **Booking rule:** book within four days at most. Same day or next day is best for show and close rates. [M4 28:06 · YT 2:49:35]

## BANT, as his team defines it

- **Budget:** enough cash flow to invest. They do not set people who cannot invest on the next call. [M4 28:13 · YT 2:49:40]
- **Authority:** can make the decision. If not, get every decision maker on the next call; if impossible, they watch the VSL and the next call is framed as a fit call. [M4 28:32 · YT 2:50:00] [M4 28:54 · YT 2:50:20]
- **Need:** a strong desired state in their words, plus a real obstacle. [M4 28:38 · YT 2:50:05]
- **Timing:** they want to solve it now. [M4 28:47 · YT 2:50:15]
- **Deal breakers:** when they hesitate, decide if it is a logistics issue or a value issue, then address that one. [M4 29:07 · YT 2:50:35] [M4 29:32 · YT 2:51:01]

## Closer: people show up, are qualified, and do not buy

It is one of three problems. [M4 45:32 · YT 3:07:01]

1. **The offer is weak.** Fix the offer, not the rep. [M4 45:36 · YT 3:07:04]
2. **They are not ready to buy.** Tells: calls run long, many questions, many calls. Fix: a VSL at every step, a multi-call agenda set up front, specific proof between calls. [M4 45:42 · YT 3:07:10]
3. **The selling skill.** Trace it to intro, disco, pitch or close. [M4 46:02 · YT 3:07:30]

| Game tape shows | Fix | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Prospect opens with "So what is this about?" | Set the agenda like a professional; do not set it apologetically | [M4 46:15 · YT 3:07:44] |
| Agenda stated, no agreement | Get the first yes ("Sound good?") while stakes are low | [M4 46:31 · YT 3:08:00] |
| Prospect re-explains what they told the setter | Read the call notes, pick up where they left off | [M4 46:46 · YT 3:08:15] |
| Current and desired state are vague | Get numbers and emotion; go one or two levels below the first answer | [M4 47:19 · YT 3:08:48] [M4 47:33 · YT 3:09:01] |
| Rep does most of the talking in discovery | Only ask questions; dig deeper | [M4 48:33 · YT 3:10:01] |
| Problem left abstract ("sales are down") | Attach money, a quantity, a timeline or a person to it | [M4 49:30 · YT 3:10:58] |
| Pitch runs past two minutes | One analogy or story, map only to their stated problems, no extra features | [M4 49:39 · YT 3:11:08] |
| Keeps talking after the ask | Ask, then stop. "Whoever talks first after the ask gets sold." | [M4 50:21 · YT 3:11:50] [M4 50:33 · YT 3:12:01] |
| Answers "I need to think about it" directly | Isolate first: confirm value, then "What's your main concern?" | [M4 50:43 · YT 3:12:10] |
| Handles one objection, then stops | Expect several. Handle, ask again, repeat. | [M4 51:09 · YT 3:12:38] |
| Argues or tries to be right | An objection is a buying signal. Agree first, use "and", not "but" (AAA), keep the same tone | [M4 51:32 · YT 3:13:01] [M4 51:51 · YT 3:13:19] |

- Closer one-on-ones use a scorecard for each part of the script and work on the weakest part with "if you do this, then that happens". [M4 54:13 · YT 3:15:40] [M4 54:34 · YT 3:16:02]
